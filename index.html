HOW TO PLAY

The goal has 6 zones (3 across, 2 high), numbered 1–6: top row left to right, then the bottom row.

123456

Coin toss: both sides play rock-paper-scissors. The winner chooses to shoot or keep first.

Shooter picks a zone to aim at and keeper picks a zone to dive to. You have 7 seconds; if time runs out your highlighted zone (or a random one) is used. Picks stay hidden until both are in.

Same zone = SAVED. Different zone = GOAL.

Power: SOFT is slow and never misses, but a keeper diving to a neighbouring zone can sometimes reach it. HARD is fast but can sail wide or hit the woodwork, and sometimes blasts through a correct dive.

Curve: bends the ball in flight. It looks great but carries a small miss risk, and a curler can swerve away from a correct dive. Top-row shots miss a bit more often.

Each side takes the chosen number of kicks, alternating. Most goals wins and the match ends early once one side can't be caught. A tie goes to sudden death.

Online: create a room, share the code, and friends pick a side. Up to two per side for 2v2; shooters and keepers rotate within a team.

Keys: 1–6 pick a zone · Q W E power · Z X C curve · arrows move · Enter locks · Space speeds up a kick.
